# Learning Process
## 1. What is the Feynman Technique?
- The Feynman Technique is a learning technique that helps you understand a concept deeply by explaining it to others in simple words.

## 2. In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?
- For me, the most interesting idea is the idea of testing yourself instead of rereading the concept or topic. We can recall and explain what we have learned without looking at the page, and we can strengthen our memory.

## 3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?
- Active mode is when we concentrate fully on a particular problem.
- Diffuse mode is when our brain relaxes and thinks freely without concentrating on the problem.
