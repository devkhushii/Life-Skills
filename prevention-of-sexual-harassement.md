# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

- Unwanted touching or physical contact.
- Making inappropriate sexual comments or jokes.
- Making uncomfortable gestures.
- Sending unwanted sexual messages, photos, or videos.
- Asking for sexual favours.
- Making unwanted requests for dates.
- Passing comments about someone's body or appearance.
- Repeatedly behaving in a way that makes someone uncomfortable.

## 2. What would you do if you face or witness any incident of such behaviour?

- Clearly tell the person to stop.
- Support the affected person and make them feel safe.
- Report the incident to HR or ICC.
- Keep evidence such as messages, emails, or screenshots.
- Never blame or judge the affected person.
- Keep the matter private.
- Never ignore or encourage such behaviour.
