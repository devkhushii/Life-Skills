# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

- Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts.
- Focus on the speaker and the topic.
- Do not interrupt the other person while they are speaking.
- Maintain eye contact and use positive body language.
- Take notes if necessary.
- Use door openers to encourage the speaker to share more.
- Ask questions whenever something is unclear.


## 2. According to Fisher's Model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

- Listen carefully to what the speaker is saying.
- Try to understand the speaker's emotions and feelings behind their words.
- Do not judge or interrupt the speaker.
- Pay attention to both verbal and non-verbal communication.
- Respond in a way that shows you understand the speaker.
- Ask questions if you do not understand something.
- Repeat or rephrase important points to confirm your understanding.


## 3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?

- Sometimes I get distracted by my own thoughts.
- I sometimes start thinking about what to reply instead of focusing on what the other person is saying.
- Background noise or other distractions can also affect my concentration.
- Sometimes I lose focus when the conversation is too long or not interesting.


## 4. What can you do to improve your listening?

- I can avoid distractions and focus completely on the speaker.
- I should avoid thinking about my response while the other person is speaking.
- I can ask questions whenever I have doubts.



## 5. When do you switch to Passive Communication Style in your day-to-day life?

- When I am new to a group of people, I usually stay quiet and observe others.
- Sometimes I avoid sharing my opinions because I am not sure how others will react.
- When I do not want to create an argument or conflict, I may choose to stay silent.


## 6. When do you switch into Aggressive Communication Style in your day-to-day life?

- Sometimes when I get frustrated or angry, I speak in a loud or rude tone.
- When someone repeatedly ignores my point, I may become impatient.


## 7. When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive Communication Style (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) in your day-to-day life?

- Sometimes when I feel hurt or angry, I avoid saying it directly.
- I may use sarcasm or taunts instead of openly expressing my feelings.
- Sometimes I become quiet or give silent treatment when I am upset with someone.
- Instead of discussing the problem, I may expect the other person to understand what is wrong.

- I should learn to recognize and names what i need and feel.
- I should express my thoughts, opinions, and feelings clearly and confidently.
- I should learn to say "NO" when necessary without feeling guilty.
